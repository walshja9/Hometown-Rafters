# Hometown Rafters: Code, Logic and Story Review

*2026-10-19. Scope: every Rhode Island Reds doc (bible plus 16 subsystem docs) and the St. Louis Perfectos choice tree. Four parallel reviews covered Reds story and continuity, Reds mechanics and buildability, Reds history and representation, and St. Louis. About 25 of their quoted lines were spot-checked against the files and all matched. Roughly 100 findings are condensed below. Paths are relative to `cities/rhode-island-reds/` unless marked `STL:`, which means `cities/st-louis-perfectos/systems/choice-tree.md`. "Bible" means `franchise-bible-complete.md`.*

## Verdict

The writing is the real asset. Gus, Danny, Walt, the Arrudas and the Longest Game are specific, earned and grounded. The Longest Game in particular matches the historical record on every number checked. The design ideas hold up too: a hidden meter read through the world, corruption felt as loss of control rather than as a number, and culture becoming currency.

What doesn't hold up is the status line. The README says "All threads closed", "stress-tested" and "No structural breaks remain", and none of those survive review.

- **Dates.** They are stated two to four ways across documents, worst around the Reckoning, the Boston Traitor and the Bandbox.
- **The middle path.** It is the least-built of the three. It has no owner, no authored Golden Age or Fall, and its identity lived in `final-polish.md`, which was never saved.
- **The mechanics.** They are blueprints, not a spec. Path, the Gus Standard and momentum (the core state) are undefined. Corruption is still the strongest strategy.
- **St. Louis.** Its dual-metric engine, the thing meant to make it a different game, currently behaves as one slider. It also needs a history pass.

Nearly all of this is text work, not redesign.

## Root cause: two copies of everything

The bible embeds near-verbatim copies of most subsystem docs. The fix docs written after it were never folded back in. Every later correction therefore landed in one copy, and the copies now disagree.

| Share of lines copied verbatim in the bible | Docs |
|---|---|
| 97–100% | `choice-tree`, `era-map-championships`, `diegetic-standard-corruption`, `simulation-layer-notes`, `golden-age-legends`, `late-era-legends`, `mike-duarte`, `danny-couture`, `research/historical-reference` |
| 0–1% (newer, never merged) | `structural-fixes-audit`, `fatal-flaw-fixes`, `longest-game`, `ownership-lineage`, `walt-callahan-broadcaster`, `boston-traitor-and-arruda-family`, `gus-almeida` |

**Fix:** cut the bible down to a spine of about 3–5K words. It would keep the premise, the three paths, one canonical timeline table, the era table and a precedence rule ("the subsystem doc wins"), and link to each subsystem doc instead of copying it. About 25K duplicated words go away, and every later fix lands in exactly one place. Do this first, or every item below has to be fixed twice.

## Fix order (top 10)

1. **Collapse the duplication** (above).
2. **One canonical Reds timeline.** Put every owner, legend and stadium date in a single table in the bible spine and make the subsystem docs agree with it. That one table resolves the Reckoning, Boston Traitor, Bandbox, Sal/Walt and Nephew findings.
3. **Rewrite `final-polish.md` and build the middle path.** That means Gus's death scene, the Compromise foil, a Slow Bleed owner and plea, Reluctant Farewell choices, and a middle-path column in every ownership profile.
4. **Define the state model** before writing more mechanics. "Path" is three drift scalars plus top-tier flags. The Gus Standard is one hidden 0–100 value with four named bands. Momentum has weights.
5. **Close the corruption-wins exploits:** title windows, Bank-then-Refuse, the uncapped Pipeline Premium and the contract-seizure test.
6. **Make Burn the Benches always happen**, with the player choosing *what* burns. This fixes a story contradiction and a no-downside choice at once.
7. **Run a Reds history and representation pass.** Mostly one-line fixes: chouriço, coffee milk, Dead Ball, Del's, Cianci, the Abruzzi leagues, I-195. Then broaden the corruption thread beyond Italian-Americans.
8. **Fix the St. Louis engine** before writing Eras 4–7. Era 1 contradicts its own path definitions, the two metrics never diverge, and Heresy dominates Era 2.
9. **Run a St. Louis history pass:** Sunday ball, 1944 seating, free agents, the "I-64 Series", talk radio, population. Also decide whether Anheuser-Busch and the Cardinals are real or fictional in this universe.
10. **Correct the status lines** in README and bible Part Ten to say what's actually done.
